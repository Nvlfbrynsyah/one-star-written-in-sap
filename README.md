A houseplant leaves its human owner a one-star review. The grievance is the annual bathtub repotting — a ritual the plant has memorized down to the radio station — and the single star is explained, in photosynthetic terms, at the end. One line, under 280 characters, written in sap.

> One star. You repot me in the bathtub every year with a radio on. I am a plant. I do not forget. I have photosynthesized six years of your guilt into sugar.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Writes the review (plus one trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output`).
